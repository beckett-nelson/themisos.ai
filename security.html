Security & Data Handling

Your client's documents
stay yours.

ThemisOS is built for plaintiff attorneys. We understand that the documents you upload carry privilege, confidentiality obligations, and real consequences. This page explains exactly how your data is handled — no vague assurances.

🗑️
Zero data retention
Uploaded documents are processed in memory and never stored on our servers. Nothing persists after your session ends.
⚖️
Privilege protected
We treat every document as attorney work product. ThemisOS does not review, log, or retain the content of your uploads.
🚫
Never used for training
Your documents and outputs are not used to train any AI model — ours or anyone else's. We have no interest in your client data beyond processing your request.
🔒
No third-party sharing
Your data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. The only external service in the pipeline is the Anthropic API, governed by enterprise-grade terms.
How your data moves

When you upload a document, here is the exact sequence:

StepWhat happens
UploadYour PDF is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS directly to our processing backend. It is never written to disk.
ExtractionText is extracted from the document in memory using our pipeline, then sent to the Anthropic API for analysis.
AnalysisAnthropic's Claude model processes the text and returns results. Under Anthropic's API terms, your data is not used for model training.
DeliveryResults are returned to your browser session. No copy is retained on our end.
Session endAll in-memory data is cleared. Nothing is logged, cached, or recoverable after you close the session.
Powered by Anthropic's API

ThemisOS uses Anthropic's Claude API as its AI backbone. Anthropic is one of the most rigorously safety-focused AI companies operating today, and their API terms provide strong contractual data protections.

Anthropic API Data Policy

Under Anthropic's standard API usage policy, data submitted via the API is not used to train or improve Anthropic's models. Anthropic maintains enterprise-grade infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest. You can review their policy directly at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

This means your documents benefit from two layers of protection: ThemisOS's own zero-retention architecture, and Anthropic's contractual commitment not to train on API data. The two together are more protective than most general-purpose legal research tools currently on the market.

What we do collect

We believe in being specific. Here is the limited information ThemisOS collects:

DataWhy

We do not run analytics that track document content, use cookies for advertising, or share any information with data brokers.
