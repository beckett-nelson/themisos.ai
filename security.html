01

Your documents are yours.
Full stop.

When you upload policy documents, complaints, correspondence, or any other materials to ThemisOS, those files exist for one purpose only: completing the analysis you asked for. They are not indexed, catalogued, stored in a database, or reviewed by anyone other than the team you've authorized to work on your matter.

We built ThemisOS for plaintiff attorneys. We understand that the documents you upload may be the most sensitive materials in an active case. We treat them accordingly.

  • Session-scoped processing. Documents are processed in isolated environments tied to your active session. When the session closes, document content is not retained.
  • No long-term storage by default. Unless you and ThemisOS have separately agreed in writing to retain materials for a multi-session engagement, files are not stored beyond the active session.
  • Encrypted in transit. All document uploads and data transmission occur over TLS-encrypted connections. Your files are never transmitted in plain text.
  • No third-party access. We do not share, sell, license, or otherwise provide your documents or their contents to any third party — for any reason, ever.
02

We never use your documents
to train AI models.

This is non-negotiable and applies without exception. The materials you share with ThemisOS — policy documents, litigation filings, demand letters, adjuster correspondence, or anything else — are never used to improve, fine-tune, or train any AI model, whether that's our own systems or any third-party model provider we work with.

  • Zero-training guarantee. Your document content is used solely to produce the output you requested. It is not fed back into any training pipeline.
  • Applies to all model providers. Where ThemisOS uses third-party AI infrastructure, we use APIs with contractual data processing agreements that prohibit training on customer data.
  • No behavioral profiling. We do not analyze your query patterns, matter types, or usage behavior for any commercial purpose.

The carrier on the other side of your case does not get smarter because you used ThemisOS. Your documents work for you — not for anyone else.

03

Attorney-client privilege
is protected by design.

ThemisOS is built for attorneys, which means privilege is not an afterthought — it's a foundational design constraint. Every workflow, every data handling decision, and every third-party integration is evaluated against the question: does this preserve or risk attorney-client privilege?

Privilege Framework

ThemisOS operates as a tool under attorney direction.

The platform does not give legal advice, does not establish independent legal relationships, and does not communicate with your clients. You direct the analysis. You own the output. ThemisOS is an extension of your work product — not a separate actor in your matter.

  • Attorney-directed engagement only. ThemisOS does not operate autonomously on your files. Every analysis is run at your direction, by your team or directly with our founder.
  • Output is work product. The recovery memos, coverage analyses, and cross-examination sequences ThemisOS generates are produced for and owned by the attorney who commissioned them.
  • No external disclosure. ThemisOS will not disclose, summarize, or reference the contents of your matter to any party outside the engagement — including in marketing, case studies, or public materials — without your explicit written consent.
  • Confidentiality by default. All inquiries, matter details, and client information shared during onboarding or in the course of an engagement are treated as confidential from the moment of first contact.
04

What we never do.

Some commitments are best stated plainly. Here is an explicit list of things ThemisOS does not do — not as a matter of policy preference, but as a hard constraint on how this platform operates.

  • ✕ Sell, rent, license, or transfer your data or document contents to any third party
  • ✕ Use your documents to train, fine-tune, or benchmark any AI model
  • ✕ Retain document content after a session ends without explicit written agreement
  • ✕ Reference, summarize, or disclose your matter in any public or marketing context
  • ✕ Contact your clients or opposing parties for any reason
  • ✕ Use tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or cookies on this website
  • ✕ Provide your contact information to any sales list or third-party service
05

Questions or custom
data agreements.

Enterprise clients, law firms with specific data governance requirements, or attorneys who need a written data processing agreement before engagement are welcome to request one. We will work with you or your IT/compliance team to meet your firm's requirements.

If your bar association, malpractice insurer, or firm policy requires specific data handling representations before using AI tools, reach out. We will put it in writing.
