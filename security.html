01

Your documents are yours.
Full stop.

When you upload policy documents, complaints, correspondence, or any other materials to ThemisOS, those files exist for one purpose only: completing the analysis you asked for. They are not indexed, catalogued, or kept in a document store, and they are never reviewed by anyone outside the engagement your firm has authorized.

We built ThemisOS for plaintiff attorneys. We understand that the documents you upload may be the most sensitive materials in an active case. We treat them accordingly.

In-memory processing. Uploaded documents are processed in memory to produce your analysis, then discarded. No copy of your original file is written to our storage.
Documents discarded, results saved to your workspace. We do not retain the files you upload. The analysis results we generate are saved to your firm's workspace so you can return to them — and you can request their deletion at any time.
Encrypted in transit. All uploads and data transmission occur over TLS-encrypted connections. Your files are never transmitted in plain text.
02

We never use your documents
to train AI models.

This is non-negotiable and applies without exception. The materials you share with ThemisOS — policy documents, litigation filings, demand letters, adjuster correspondence, or anything else — are never used to improve, fine-tune, or train any AI model, whether that's our own systems or any third-party model provider we work with.

Zero-training commitment. Your document content is used solely to produce the output you requested. It is not fed into any training pipeline.
Applies to our model provider. Document text is processed through Anthropic's commercial API, whose terms prohibit training on your inputs and outputs and delete them within 30 days.
No behavioral profiling. We do not mine your query patterns, matter types, or usage behavior for any commercial purpose.

The carrier on the other side of your case does not get smarter because you used ThemisOS. Your documents work for you — not for anyone else.

03

Powered by Anthropic —
enterprise-grade infrastructure.

ThemisOS runs on Anthropic's Claude API — one of the most rigorously safety-focused AI systems available. This means your documents benefit from two independent layers of protection: ThemisOS's own architecture, which discards uploads after analysis, and Anthropic's contractual commitment not to train on API data.

Anthropic API Data Policy

Under Anthropic's standard commercial API terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train or improve Anthropic's models and is deleted from Anthropic's systems within 30 days. Anthropic maintains enterprise-grade infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest. You can review their policy directly at anthropic.com/legal/privacy →

This two-layer structure — our architecture plus Anthropic's contractual guarantees — provides stronger protection than most general-purpose research tools currently on the market. Unlike consumer AI products, API usage is governed by explicit data processing terms, not general privacy policies written for mass audiences.

How your data moves

StepWhat happens
UploadYour file is transmitted over an encrypted (TLS) connection to our processing backend.
ExtractionText is extracted from the document in memory, then sent to the Anthropic API for analysis.
AnalysisAnthropic's Claude model processes the text and returns results. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, your data is not used for model training and is deleted within 30 days.
DeliveryResults are returned to you and saved to your firm's workspace so you can revisit them.
CleanupThe uploaded document is discarded from memory — no copy of your original file is stored. Saved results remain available until you request their deletion.

Beyond Anthropic, ThemisOS relies on a small set of infrastructure subprocessors for hosting, database, billing, and email. The complete list — along with our full data-retention terms — is set out in our Privacy Policy.

04

What we never do.

Some commitments are best stated plainly. Here is an explicit list of things ThemisOS does not do — not as a matter of policy preference, but as a hard constraint on how this platform operates.

Sell, rent, license, or transfer your data or document contents to any third party
Use your documents to train, fine-tune, or benchmark any AI model
Retain your uploaded documents after the analysis completes
Reference, summarize, or disclose your matter in any public or marketing context
Contact your clients or opposing parties for any reason
Build advertising or behavioral profiles from your activity
Provide your contact information to any sales list or third-party service
