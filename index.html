How it works

From raw documents to
recovery strategy

CoverageIQ processes your full document stack and builds a unified understanding of every coverage layer — in a single pipeline.

01
Document ingestion & OCR
Upload PDFs, scans, or Word files. Handles multi-column layouts, endorsement schedules, and stamped amendments with 99.2% fidelity.
02
Legal-language analysis
Trained on decades of insurance case law. Identifies exclusions, conditions precedent, and trigger clauses — and flags ambiguities courts have ruled on.
03
Coverage tower reconstruction
Maps every primary, excess, umbrella, and D&O layer into a visual policy tower, tracing attachment points, limits, and gaps across carriers and years.
04
Conflict & gap detection
Compares policies against each other and your litigation record. Anti-stacking clauses, pro-rata disputes, and follow-form conflicts surface automatically.
05
Recovery memo generation
Outputs a cite-ready brief with theory of recovery, applicable limits, exposure summary, and recommended demand — ready for counsel to file or negotiate.
