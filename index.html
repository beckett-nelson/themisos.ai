How it works

From raw documents to recovery strategy

Themis processes your full document stack and builds a unified understanding of every coverage layer — giving plaintiff counsel the analytical leverage carriers spend years building against you.

01
Document ingestion & OCR
Upload PDFs, scans, or Word files. Handles multi-column layouts, endorsement schedules, and stamped amendments with high fidelity.
02
Legal-language analysis
Trained on decades of insurance case law. Identifies exclusions, conditions precedent, and trigger clauses — and flags ambiguities courts have ruled on.
03
Coverage tower reconstruction
Maps every primary, excess, umbrella, and D&O layer into a visual policy tower, tracing attachment points and gaps across carriers and years.
04
Conflict & gap detection
Compares policies against each other and your litigation record. Anti-stacking clauses, pro-rata disputes, and follow-form conflicts surface automatically.
05
Recovery memo & cross-exam output
Outputs a cite-ready brief with theory of recovery, applicable limits, and cross-examination question lines — ready for counsel to file, negotiate, or take to deposition.
